Your journeys,
in motion.

Every photograph you keep already knows where and when it was taken. Stourk reads only that, and flies the stork along your years, city by city, on a map that never leaves your iPhone.

The journey

Pick the years.
Start the journey.

Choose a stretch of your life on the ticket: a year, a summer, or all of it. Then start the journey.

The flight

The Stourk flies your years.

The map glides from city to city while the stork draws the line between them. Every trip leaves its ink, and by the end your years are one route across the board.

The end card

A boarding pass
for your life.

When the stork lands you get a boarding pass: days travelled, cities, distance, and a barcode that is really your calendar, one cluster of bars for every day you took a photograph. Save it as a poster or export the whole flight as a video.

The post

Every city writes you a letter.

After the flight the post arrives. One envelope for every city you have been to, addressed to you. Tear it open and the city's own note comes out first, then your photographs as prints.

Open the envelope

Every city has something to say to you. What it wrote is for the one who tears the envelope.

Then the prints

Behind the note, your photographs from that city, dated on the foot. Tap one to hold it up.

The prints

Your photographs,
as prints.

Each print comes straight from your library, in the order you took the photographs. Nothing is copied anywhere else.

The passport

A passport, stamped
by your travels.

Twelve engraved pages, a stamp for every place you have been, and a surprise for anyone who tilts it. Turn the pages the way you would with a real one.

Labels & the case

Labels earned
on the road.

One label for every place you go, dealt to you one at a time. They live on your suitcase, next to the letters and the passport, and the case fills up as you travel.

Poster

Print the flight

The finished map and its boarding pass, saved as a poster straight from the screen.

Video

Export the reel

The whole journey rendered on your iPhone as a video. While it prints, Stourk deals out what it found: the two ends, the four corners, the longest leg.

Years

Any stretch you like

One summer, one decade, or everything since the first photograph. Facts and labels follow the years you picked.

Privacy

What stays on your iPhone: everything.

  • Stourk reads the date and location stored with your photographs, and nothing else about you.
  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs watching you.
  • Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors, like any map app.
  • Clear the index whenever you like. Your photographs are never touched.
